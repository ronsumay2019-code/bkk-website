BEFORE YOU MINE

A better fit starts with a question.

How do I choose my size?

Our overall range is XL to 5XL. Sizes vary by piece, so ask our host for the garment’s measurements and compare them with a comfortable item you own.

Where can I see prices and available pieces?

Visit our Facebook page for live showcases. Our team confirms pricing, colors, sizing, and availability for the piece you’re interested in.

Can I order directly from this website?

We take orders through our Facebook Live and Messenger. Message the official page for help reserving a piece and confirming your order.
