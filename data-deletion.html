Data deletion

How to request deletion of customer information from Bangkok Plus Size OOTD.

Overview

At Bangkok Plus Size OOTD, we respect your privacy rights and provide transparent mechanisms for customers to request the deletion of their personal information, through our customer support team.

What Customer Data We Hold

Because we do not have user accounts or web checkouts on this site, the only personal information we process consists of information voluntarily provided during or after our Facebook Live broadcasts:

  • Customer recipient name
  • Philippine delivery address
  • Contact phone number
  • Past chat history and payment confirmation screenshots inside Facebook Messenger

How to Request Data Deletion

If you wish to have your shipping information or contact records permanently removed from our active customer contact records, please follow these steps:

  1. Send a Deletion Request: Send “Data Deletion Request” in a direct message to our official Facebook Page at m.me/bangkokplussizeootd.
  2. Provide Identifying Details: To locate your records, include:
    • Your Facebook account name / Messenger profile handle
    • The recipient contact phone number used for previous orders
  3. Processing & Confirmation: Our team will verify your request, delete your shipping and contact information from our active order records after reviewing your request, and send you a confirmation message once completed.

Legal Record-Keeping Exceptions

Please note that basic transaction invoices and proof of completed shipments may need to be retained to meet applicable record-keeping obligations.

Questions?

For any questions concerning data handling, message our official page.
